JDM Kingdom eBay Tool – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the JDM Kingdom eBay Tool (the “Tool”) handles information when connecting to eBay services and APIs.

Information we access

When an authorised eBay account connects to the Tool, the Tool may access information made available through eBay APIs, including seller account information, listings, inventory, orders and fulfilment data, sales and performance metrics, advertising and marketing performance, and related identifiers required to operate the integration.

How we use information

Information is used to operate and improve the Tool, analyse JDM Kingdom’s eBay store performance, monitor listings and inventory, evaluate sales and advertising performance, optimise listings and campaigns, identify operational issues, and conduct marketplace and product research where permitted by eBay.

Data sharing and sale

We do not sell personal information obtained through eBay APIs. Information may be processed by service providers used to operate the Tool, such as hosting, database, logging, and security providers, only as necessary to provide the service and subject to applicable obligations.

Data retention and security

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, legal or compliance obligations, and legitimate business needs. Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

eBay account deletion requests

The Tool maintains an endpoint for eBay Marketplace Account Deletion notifications. When eBay notifies us that an applicable user has requested deletion of personal data, we will process the request in accordance with eBay requirements and applicable law, including deleting or anonymising covered personal data where required.

OAuth and credentials

eBay OAuth credentials and tokens are used only to authenticate authorised API access. Secret credentials are not intended to be publicly disclosed and are stored using appropriate security controls.

Your choices

An authorised eBay user may revoke the Tool’s access through eBay or request that stored information associated with the integration be deleted where applicable.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the Tool changes or as required by eBay or applicable law. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.

Contact

For privacy questions relating to this Tool, contact JDM Kingdom through its official eBay seller contact channel.

This policy applies to the JDM Kingdom eBay Tool and its use of eBay APIs.
